# Data Notice

This archive contains bibliographic metadata, titles, abstracts, and public
paper links for research and indexing. It does not contain paper PDFs or other
full text. Copyright and database rights remain with the original authors,
conferences, publishers, and platforms. This repository grants no blanket
license over source metadata or abstracts; public availability here must not be
read as permission for further redistribution.

Source terms differ:

- [AAAI](https://aaai.org/about-aaai/copyright-notice/) proceedings are openly
  readable, but AAAI requires permission for republication and redistribution.
- [CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/) provides open-access paper pages while
  stating that authors or other rightsholders retain copyright.
- [Springer](https://dev.springernature.com/terms-conditions/) treats abstracts
  as copyrighted material unless the associated article carries an applicable
  open-access license.
- [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/legal/terms) applies CC0 to metadata only
  to the extent that the submitter or OpenReview holds the relevant rights;
  article licenses may differ by record.
- [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/pmlr-license-agreement.pdf) articles use
  CC BY 4.0 and require attribution to the original paper and PMLR page.
- [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/FAQ) authors retain paper copyright; the
  conference's publication license is not a general third-party redistribution
  license.
- A community aggregator, `papercopilot/paperlists` on GitHub, supplies the
  2020--2022 vintages and the accepted-paper deltas of later years (upstream
  commit pinned in each release's `source_metadata`). The aggregator ships no
  LICENSE file; the platform terms above still govern the underlying records,
  and the aggregated copy must not be redistributed beyond this local
  research repository.
- [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/policies/) supplies the publisher-
  registered enumeration for the MICCAI (Springer LNCS volumes) and ISBI
  (IEEE) proceedings releases: DOIs, titles, authors, and landing-page
  URLs. Crossref metadata is a citation-facilitation service; the underlying
  publisher terms above still govern the records, and only the enumerated
  bibliographic fields (no abstracts from Crossref) are stored.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api) supplies
  abstracts, citation counts, and open-access PDF links for the MICCAI and
  ISBI releases. S2 API data is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0: reuse is non-commercial
  only, with attribution to Semantic Scholar. The raw API fetches are stored
  under `corpus/sources/medical/` and pinned by SHA-256 in each release's
  `source_metadata`; citation counts are a point-in-time snapshot of S2's
  index as of the fetch date (2026-08-20).

Use the official URLs stored with each record to verify the current source
terms before redistributing material or using it outside research and indexing.
